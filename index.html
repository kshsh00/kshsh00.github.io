$ 01 // METHODOLOGY & TOOLKIT

Always curious. Focused on safer software.

Hi, I’m Kavish. My focus has always been on how real applications break — specifically web, API, and thick-client systems where authentication, authorization, or access-control checks fail. I want security to be something teams genuinely understand and prioritize, so I focus on clear, practical findings instead of noisy scanner dumps.

I like to keep myself sharp by hunting for bugs on HackerOne, solving Linux, Windows, and web CTF challenges on Hack The Box, and building custom Burp Suite workflows and Python PoC scripts. Whether I’m checking third-party TLS configurations, working with developers to retest a patch, or writing business-impact reports for non-technical readers, my goal is to help make applications more secure — even when the work happens behind the scenes.
